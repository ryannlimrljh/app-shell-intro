What is modelled, and why

The split across your pods. The deck reports target, booked and forecast at Head of Sales level and stops. There is no per-pod or per-person figure anywhere in this cut, so the pod rows hold your real total and invent only the division between them.

The division is deterministic, so the same pod always draws the same picture, and the rows always sum back to your stated total.

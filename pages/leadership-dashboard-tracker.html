What this dashboard can't tell you yet

Ranked by leverage

Each item was asked for in review and is deliberately not built — no confirmed dataset supports it. Ranked by how many open questions each source closes. Switch to Mode 2 to see the board with all six landed.

1

Pipeline by deal stage — negotiation, verbal, contracted-not-booked

The highest-leverage single source: it turns attainment from a rear-view mirror into a forecast, says whether Daniel's 85% is a thin pipeline or slow closes, and replaces the run-rate projection with a real commit/best-case. One source, three answers.

Unlocks: forecast · rep health · blip-vs-trend certainty
2

Margin or gross profit by advertiser and product

Changes which revenue is worth chasing. Lazada at RM 1.8M and ZUS at RM 0.6M may rank differently on contribution than on spend — and it makes concentration meaningful, since concentrated and low-margin is materially worse than concentrated alone.

Unlocks: revenue quality · true account ranking
3

Sellable inventory vs sold — TV, Radio, Digital

Resolves the loudest anomaly on this board. Digital at 75% is either a demand problem or a supply and rate-card constraint. Those need opposite responses, and nothing here distinguishes them.

Unlocks: the Digital 75% diagnosis
4

Churn reason codes on lapsed accounts

Turns the lapsed list from a bill into a playbook. We can already see all four lapsed in Q1 — a reason code says whether that was budget cycle, price, service or competitive loss, which is the difference between a win-back call and a pricing review.

Unlocks: win-back strategy
5

Total revenue by advertiser, not just new and lapsed

Smallest ask, largest unlock per unit of effort. The top-20 lists only cover accounts entering or leaving. One full advertiser-level table completes account concentration, so "top 5 = X% of revenue" becomes answerable instead of approximated by segment.

Unlocks: account concentration · dependency risk
6

Market adspend benchmark — external

Says whether +6.4% is a win or a quiet loss of share. The only item needing a purchased third-party source rather than an internal pull, which is why it ranks last on feasibility despite mattering strategically.

Unlocks: growth vs market · share trend

Sold against what we had to sell

Sold against sellable, with target attainment beneath

source 2 inventory

The solid bar is what we sold; the pale remainder is shelf we never filled. Digital sold 62% of its inventory and reached 75% of target — the stock was there, so it is a demand problem. Addressable is the opposite at 97% sold out, so its 109% is capped by supply. Sell Digital harder; buy more addressable. Across all four lines we sold RM 148.2M of RM 174.0M sellable.

Revenue against contribution

14 accounts — bubble size is gross profit, the ranking that matters

source 4 margin

Two of the four biggest profit contributors are sub-RM 1M accounts. 99 Speedmart bills RM 0.85M at 44% margin and out-earns Lazada's RM 1.80M on contribution. The whole green band — 99 Speedmart, Secret Recipe, ZUS and Tealive just beneath it — sits in the bottom-left of any spend-ranked report and would never be prioritised. Revenue rank and profit rank are different league tables.

Us against the market

Indexed growth, and the share that follows from it

source 1 benchmark

The most uncomfortable panel here, and the reason to buy the data. Growing 6.4% in a market growing 8.1% means share fell from 14.2% to 13.8% — four tenths of a point, or about RM 4.3M of revenue that existed and went elsewhere. Every other widget on this board calls this a good year. Only this one calls it a loss.

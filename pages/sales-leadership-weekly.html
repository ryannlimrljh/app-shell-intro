How the numbers are built

Forecast is booked plus open pipeline weighted by the source Win Rate field, on the Net basis carried in the NeoCRM export. Deals already contracted are removed from pipeline upstream, so booked and pipeline do not overlap.

Deals scored at zero per cent win rate are treated as lost and excluded entirely: 175 rows, RM15.1M of planned budget.

80%+ opportunity is open pipeline at the 80 per cent win rate, before weighting and excluding won deals. It is headroom, not forecast.

Targets below Head of Sales are a placeholder. Every seller has one of their own, set from the clients they carry and re-cut each quarter by their head and manager. Those figures exist; they are not in the 7 August export. Everything on this board at pod and person level is a stand-in until they arrive.

Where the comparison breaks

Digital and AA close on the 23rd. Their August figures cover 24 July to 23 August and hold 14 of 31 delivery days at this cut, so their booked figures are not comparable with TV and Radio and will catch up as the period closes.

vs LY is gross against a net forecast, so it understates current performance throughout.

Forward contracted revenue exists for TV and Radio only. The AA and Digital billing extracts carry August alone, so their forward book is missing rather than zero.

Last week the current month was July, so month-on-month is not like for like. Week on week starts once the history workbook is live.

Forward deal values are the full campaign as carried in the export, which runs July 2026 to January 2027 and cannot show earlier months. Ranking by full value is deliberate, so a deal worth more than it looks inside the window gets early intervention.
